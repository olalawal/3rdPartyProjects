Brief Description of Courses:

·         Language courses: Level 1 courses provide introduction to speaking, reading, and writing in the language and basic vocabulary. In this course, you will learn how to speak, read, and write and carry out simple conversations in everyday situations and contexts. Students will also learn about basic aspects of Yoruba/Twi culture to navigate through various cultural situations at the basic level. Level 2 courses is a continuation of the Level 1 course, which involves more advanced vocabulary and syntax. Students will continue to develop further in speaking, reading, and writing Yoruba/Twi and acquiring proficiency in communicating in Yoruba/Twi in more situations and contexts not covered in Level 1.

 

·         African Culture Course: This course takes students through the history of relevant West African countries, the major ethnicities, their traditional customs and traditions and their modern-day equivalents. Topics covered will include family life, food, marriage, religion, festivals, traditional, traditional music, etc. Students will also learn how to express basic facts about themselves in an African language by learning some basic vocabulary in the language.  It’s a perfect course for anyone wanting to know about other cultures and people around in Africa.
